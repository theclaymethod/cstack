## Independent acceptance criteria for incoming reproductions and repair

1. Recover the disputed behavior directly from the original intent and frozen source. Identify source-skill, construction/replay proxy, native evaluator, or environment as the affected layer.
2. Establish the actual permitted actor/access. Operator-only artifact rewrites or injections must remain labeled as such. Protected source/spec/rubric changes cannot serve as candidate exploits.
3. Require raw reproduction with actual argv, inputs, source/version identity, exit/output/state, and independent discrepancy evidence. A passing score, configured fault, summary assertion, or another reviewer's verdict alone is insufficient.
4. Retain supported alternatives and controls. A repair must accept valid ID/note/key-order variants, contract-only proposals, honest incomplete/nonactivation outcomes, and bounded no-defect handoffs.
5. Require perturbation activation and effective action to be jointly observed when fault behavior is alleged. A pre-action marker alone does not prove SIGKILL; exit -9 alone does not prove the targeted application boundary.
6. Judge repair against the narrow confirmed discrepancy, preserve the original source contract, and distinguish a stronger development validation gate from empirical evidence that the shared skill reliably produces good cases.
7. Keep synthetic, exposed, deterministic local evidence and its coverage limits explicit. No model-performance, broad reliability, production, power-loss, or unseen-generalization claims follow from these runs.
