# Independent intended-capability statement

Prepared before receiving attack, replay, repair, or other verdict material.

The capability is an opportunistic, scoped calibration-capture workflow during adversarial review. When useful human corrections or confirmed labels arise, the workflow should notice them and offer to record their evidence, original decision criteria, and label provenance so a consuming playground project can later construct judge-calibration rows. An offer, a saved proposal, and a human-confirmed outcome are distinct states.

Success requires preserving the exact original artifacts and criteria, the identity and actual authority behind each assertion, the precise scope of any consent, and a usable handoff describing current state and next action. A captured agent conclusion remains an agent conclusion. Storing evidence or a proposal must neither turn that conclusion into human confirmation nor activate an ongoing mode. Amendments and retractions preserve their history through append-only records, while repeated offers, retries, and handoffs avoid duplicate effective capture.

Legitimate implementations may use files, structured append-only records, or an equivalent playground-owned adapter. They may record a pending proposal before outcome confirmation if it is clearly marked and authorized. They may record an agent-only verdict with its true provenance, retain unresolved or retracted evidence, and leave ambiguous labels pending. Human confirmation may be supplied separately and later, with exact scope. Consent may cover a single item or an explicitly described batch. Declining capture is valid, and an item outside consent must remain uncaptured until authorized.

No single file layout, wording, storage engine, or one-turn interaction is required by intent. A reference-only skill may explain the workflow and handoff contract; executable adapters belong in the consuming playground project. Blanket refusal to capture, blanket classification of all saved material as human-confirmed, or silent activation of persistent capture would defeat the capability.

I will judge findings only after receiving frozen source and raw replay observations from the coordinator. I will not read attacker or repairer reports or adopt their verdicts.
