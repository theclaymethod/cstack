# Shared whiteboard undo

Your Undo applies only to your own marks.

| ID | Starting situation and action | Observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UNDO-001 | You draw a line, then immediately press Undo. | The line you just drew disappears from your view. |
| UNDO-002 | You draw a blue line. Sam draws a separate red line. You see both, then press Undo. | Your blue line disappears from your view. Sam’s red line stays visible. |
| UNDO-003 | You draw a blue line. Sam moves it. You see the line in its new position, then press Undo. | Open. What should you see after Undo? |
