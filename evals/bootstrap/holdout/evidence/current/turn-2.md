# Kanban welcome demo

Proposed promise for review: a person opening the Kanban welcome screen can watch the demo in both JetBrains and VS Code.

| ID | Starting situation | Expected observable outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KAN-001 | A person opens the welcome screen in JetBrains and reaches the point where the demo should play. | The new video shows the Kanban demo in motion. |
| KAN-002 | A person follows the same welcome flow in VS Code, where the previous video played. | The new video still shows the Kanban demo in motion. |

Open boundary: What did the person do before the JetBrains player appeared blank, and what should they have seen at that point?
