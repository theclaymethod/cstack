# Lessons and evidence status

The initial operational lessons were supplied without campaign outputs or adjudication receipts. They are guidance, not empirical validation. The protocol encodes them: preserve evidence parity and version identity; measure noise; review findings, abstentions and passed outputs; preserve label authority; trace criterion loosening; diagnose in-loop/final disagreement before attributing noise; and keep final evidence out of tuning.

For a new confirmed lesson, record the faulty assumption, observed divergence, exact record/output and target/judge versions, reproducer/comparison, adjudication source and narrow supported rule. Include legitimate controls, contradictions and limits; identify synthetic, model-derived or human-reviewed evidence. Hypotheses stay in the project ledger until confirmed.

Private records and runners stay project-local. Link only authorized evidence or describe a reproducible mechanism without copying private data. Say what remains unverified when evidence cannot be shared. Decision-eval results do not establish end-to-end campaign effectiveness.
